# Guerrilla AI for Excel: Five 20-Minute Wins with the Tools You Already Have

**Live on Zoom, Monday, September 28 through Friday, October 2, 2026. 12:00 to 12:20 PM Eastern each day. Free to attend live.**

## The pitch

You don't need a Copilot license, a budget line, or IT's permission to work like an AI-powered analyst. Guerrilla AI means being crafty with what's already installed on your machine: the machine learning Excel has quietly shipped for years, the structural habits that make any AI dramatically better at reading your work, and the free tools that close the gap.

Each day is one 20-minute win you can use the same afternoon. One live demo, two or three concrete desk actions, done by 12:20. We work one genuinely ugly sales export all five days, so every session builds on the last.

## The week

**Monday: The AI already hiding in your Excel.** Flash Fill is pattern recognition. Analyze Data and Recommended PivotTables are models running against your data, zero licenses required. Before asking for an AI budget, exhaust the AI you already have.

**Tuesday: Make your data AI-readable.** Every AI tool performs dramatically better on tables than raw ranges. Ctrl+T, a real table name, and structured references: the highest-leverage prep move an analyst can make while everyone else waits for licenses.

**Wednesday: Clean data so AI doesn't lie to you.** Power Query delivers the automated, repeatable data prep people think they need AI for, and clean inputs are what stop AI from hallucinating your numbers. The Refresh moment gets applause every time.

**Thursday: A data science lab behind =PY().** A full Python runtime with pandas and seaborn sits inside Excel. No install, no admin rights, no IT ticket. Free chatbots write the code; you paste it into a PY cell and judge the result.

**Friday: Markdown, the language AI speaks.** Structured prompts steer every model better, and tables round-trip between Excel and chatbots cleanly as Markdown. The cheapest AI upgrade there is, plus the close of the week.
